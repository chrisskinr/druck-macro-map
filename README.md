# Druck Macro Map

Stanley Druckenmiller's macro framework as a live causal map. Indicators flow from policy through liquidity, the price of money, inflation, earnings and credit, and market internals, down to asset prices. The page shows a regime read, his checklist, and a plain-English commentary.

**Live page:** https://chrisskinr.github.io/druck-macro-map/

The data comes from FRED and Yahoo Finance and is a snapshot as of the date on the page. Druckenmiller quotes are verbatim and carry their source citations. The commentary is interpretation, not investment advice.
